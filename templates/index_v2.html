🔍 TypeSafe Review Analyzer

Detect fake reviews using AI-powered analysis

Step 1: Copy a Google Maps listing link

https://www.google.com/maps/place/...

Step 2: Paste into the link field

Step 3: Click "Extract & Analyze"

The app will:

  • Extract all reviews from the listing
  • Analyze each for fake indicators
  • Show suspicion scores
  • Flag likely fake reviews

💡 Requires: requests and beautifulsoup4 (pip install requests beautifulsoup4)
